# GRPO Test Prompts - Comprehensive Quality Assessment
# Use these prompts to test GRPO vs baseline generation quality

# Motion Quality Tests (5 prompts)
A ball rolling smoothly down a gentle wooden slope
A rope swinging like a pendulum with consistent rhythm  
Water flowing steadily from a faucet into a sink below
A coin spinning rapidly on a table before falling over
Autumn leaves falling from a tree, swaying gently in the breeze

# Object Interaction Tests (4 prompts)
A hand pushing a wooden block, which slides across a table
A hammer striking a nail, driving it into a wooden board
Dominoes falling in sequence, each triggering the next
Scissors cutting through a piece of paper in a straight line

# Fine Detail Tests (3 prompts)
Rain drops hitting a window pane, individual droplets clearly visible
A hummingbird hovering near a flower, wings beating rapidly
Spider web covered in morning dew, each droplet distinct

# Multi-Object Tests (3 prompts)
Three colorful balloons floating upward at different speeds
Five birds flying together in V-formation across the sky
Two people tossing a red ball back and forth between them

# Lighting and Weather Tests (3 prompts)
Sunset gradually darkening, colors shifting from orange to deep purple
A candle flame flickering, casting dancing shadows on the nearby wall
Morning fog slowly clearing to reveal a mountain landscape

# Action Completion Tests (2 prompts)
A wooden door opening smoothly from closed to wide open
An ice cube melting slowly into a puddle of clear water
